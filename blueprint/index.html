03 — THE SHORT-TERM RENTAL PORTFOLIO

Income today.
Options tomorrow.

Great vacation rentals can create cash flow while guests help pay down debt on assets your family can also enjoy. The numbers must work after management, cleaning, utilities, repairs, vacancies, insurance and reserves—not just in an Airbnb screenshot wearing its Sunday best.

Design Your Dream Lifestyle
CONTROL + LONG HORIZON

Purchase

Best when the market is proven, capital is available and you want depreciation, appreciation and maximum control.

  • Own the upside and downside
  • Full operating control
  • Higher initial capital
TEST + LOWER INITIAL CAPITAL

Lease-Option

Best when you want to validate demand before committing—but the contract must expressly allow short-term rentals.

  • Option premium can expire
  • No automatic depreciation
  • Owner/title risk remains
